erst_dev_adblocker — Ad blocker for Chrome: blocks ads and trackers, hides ad elements, and skips video ads. The interface is available in 44 languages.

• Blocks advertising and tracking requests at the network level.
• Hides banners, native ads, and sponsored blocks.
• Skips ads in YouTube, VK Video, and RuTube.
• Whitelist for sites you trust.
• 44 interface languages with search and flags.

1. Click the extension icon in the Chrome toolbar.
2. Turn blocking on or off with the switch.
3. Open the gear menu to choose a language or add a site to the whitelist.

No personal data is collected or transmitted. Settings are stored locally and deleted with the extension. No analytics, no remote code.
